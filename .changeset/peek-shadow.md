The previous page peeking in over the spine of a single-page book now drops a shadow on the page under it, so it reads as paper and not a flat bar. The fold's own shadows hug its crease, which for a peek is half a page away in the hidden half, so none of them reached the strip. It follows `shadows` and `shadowOpacity`, and a turn in hand is drawn as before.
