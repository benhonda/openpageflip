title: Right to left

import CoreExample from "../../../components/CoreExample.astro";

`binding: "right"` puts the spine on the right, which is all a right-to-left book is. The cover sits alone on the left, reading on turns a page from the left edge over to the right, and on touch a swipe to the right goes forward. Your pages stay in reading order, first to last, the same as for any other book - the binding decides which side of the spine each one lands on, not you.

The page-side classes follow the screen, so `opf-page--left` is still the page on the left. It's just that in this book that's the later one of the pair.
