OpenPageFlip

A page-turn effect for the web

Drag an edge

Hover the outer edge and it furls. Take hold and pull: let go past the middle and the page turns; let go early and it settles back.

Or just click

Click a page's outer edge and it turns. The middle is yours: text selects, links open. On a touch screen, a swipe anywhere turns the page.

Hard and soft

Covers are hard, so they swing as one stiff sheet. Inner pages bend along the fold, the way paper does.

Any HTML

Pages are ordinary elements. Text, images, forms, video - whatever a page holds keeps working.
