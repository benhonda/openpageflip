Same book, turned a quarter: the spine runs along the top, so a page lifts from its bottom edge and folds up over the one behind it. On a mouse, hover near the bottom and the edge furls to show you where to grab.

Swipe up on a phone

A quick swipe up turns the page, and a swipe down turns it back. Scrolling past the pad still works from anywhere outside it.
